Instagram follow-back

Local follow-back checker from Meta’s official export. No Instagram password. Drop your ZIP below; lists stay on this computer.

Load your export

  1. Instagram → Accounts Center → Download your information
  2. Select Followers and following only
  3. Format JSON (HTML also works)
  4. Date range All time — shorter ranges omit older followers
  5. Drop the ZIP here or choose a file
Or drag and drop onto this page. Full dumps (multi‑GB) are parsed on the local server — keep this page open via python3 app.py.
